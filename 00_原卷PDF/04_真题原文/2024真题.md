Passage 1

It was on Sunday morning, when Sam went to see his grandfather alone. He took the bus to his grandfather’s house, as it was quite a distance away. As the bus traveled along, it stopped at a busy stop and a crowd of passengers boarded, including an old lady.

The bus was already crowded, and there were no more seats available. The old lady looked tired and strained, but she held on to the handrail for support. Sam noticed this and felt a bit uneasy, but he was also far from having the empty seat and couldn’t offer one to her.

Suddenly, a voice came from the back of the bus. It was the little girl, who had boarded the bus before. “Please, take my seat, Grandma,” she said sweetly. The old lady was taken aback but soon smiled gratefully and sat down.

This act of kindness didn’t just move the old lady. Other passengers, inspired by the little girl, also got up and offered their seats to those who needed them. The atmosphere in the bus suddenly changed from one kind of indifference to one kind of kindness and compassion.

Sam watched the scene with a sense of awe. He was amazed by the little girl’s selflessness and generosity. It made him realize that true kindness comes from the heart, regardless of age or size.

As the bus continued its journey, Sam couldn’t help but think about the little girl’s act of kindness. He turned to the passenger next to him and said, “What a lovely little girl!” Indeed, her simple yet profound act of giving had left a lasting impression on everyone on the bus.

Passage 2

In my job, I get to combine my two passions: sport and writing.

I wrote newspaper articles about sport while studying for my journalism degree. I worked as a trainee writer for a local newspaper. Now, I write for a top newspaper. Today, I am writing an article about a baseball game. I arrive at the stadium two hours before the game starts. I have got ready for my work.

By now the players are arriving, so I hurry down to wait outside the changing area where I manage to get a short interview with the home team’s star player. I have limited time, so I only ask a few important questions, such as how he’s feeling and what his expectations are.

Once the game starts, I take notes on the action as it happens. It is an exciting game—The home team wins! As the fans leave, I go to the press conference with other journalists. We listen to both team coaches’ thoughts on the game and I get some good quotes.

Finally, it is time for me to pull all my notes together into the finished article to send to the newspaper. I only have a couple of hours to write it all up. The paper will be printed tonight, and the public will be reading my article over breakfast in the morning. I love sharing my love for sport through words.
